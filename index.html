Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Sahoo Freightways own its own trucks?

Sahoo Freightways is presented here as a freight coordination and connection service. We connect enquiries with independent truck owners and transport partners; this website does not claim ownership of a truck fleet.

Can I request transport anywhere in India?

You can submit enquiries for routes across India. A suitable vehicle or partner is not guaranteed and depends on route, timing and availability.

Is submitting the form a confirmed booking?

No. The form starts an enquiry only. Booking, rates, payment terms, documents, cargo handling and responsibilities must be reviewed and agreed by the relevant parties before dispatch.
